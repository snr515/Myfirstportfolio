Bio

Sarah here. I'm an occupational therapist by day and a beginner in tech by night. I earned my undergraduate degree in psychology and my graduate degree in occupational therapy.

So why am I here? In my day-to-day work, assistive technology is one of the greatest tools in our toolbox for supporting children with all types of disabilities. It can mean the difference between a child who can't communicate their wants and needs and one who can share their aspirations with you. Yet when navigating digital platforms, it isn't uncommon to run into barriers that keep people with great ideas from participating — simply because those platforms aren't accessible.

That's why I'm here: to contribute something great, a different perspective as someone who not only uses tech in my everyday routine, but uses it to give a voice to others who might not otherwise have one.

So far, I've learned basic coding and worked with semantic HTML, CSS, and responsive design.
